The filter allows users to quickly find workouts based on selected attributes.

Currently, there are three filter options, but more can be added in the future:

- Muscle group
- Difficulty
- Duration

Each option is displayed as a clickable box. 
When clicked, a checkmark appears to indicate selection. 
Boxes can also be unchecked to remove a filter.

These filters help users narrow down workouts to match their goals, 
experience level, or available time, making it easier to find suitable exercises.
